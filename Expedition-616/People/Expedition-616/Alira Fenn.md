# Alira Fenn

A member of [Expedition-616](/Expedition-616/People/Expedition-616).

**Race:** Half-Elf
**Class:** Wizard (Divination)
**Age:** 21
**Description:** Studious, quiet, wears round glasses and always scribbling in a worn notebook.
**Backstory:** Daughter of an archivist who died of sickness; Alira buried herself in ancient texts about the mainland.
