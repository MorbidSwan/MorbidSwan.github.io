# The Cataclysm

616 years ago, a great magic washed across the land of [Nala](/Expedition-616/Places/Nala), causing great fissures and eruption in the earth and Demons to rise from hell. Along with the sweeping magic, a great [pillar of light](/Expedition-616/Places/The%20Pillar) streaked up from the horizon. No one is sure what this pillar is, but every year, at dusk on the summer solstice, the [pillar of white light](/Expedition-616/Places/The%20Pillar) begins to turn red before a flash of red light consumes the land and, seemingly randomly, people begin to fade into dust. The people of [Song Town](/Expedition-616/Places/Song%20Town) call this [Juida](/Expedition-616/Events/Jiuda).
