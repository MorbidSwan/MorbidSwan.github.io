# Travel Pace

### Pace Table [^1]

| **Pace** | **Miles per Hour** | **Miles per Day** | Hours per Day [^2] | Hours per Hex [^2] | Adv/Disadv                                                     |
| -------- | ------------------ | ----------------- | ------------------ | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fast     | 4                  | 30                | 7.5                | 1.5                | Disadvantage on *Perception*, *Survival*, and *Stealth* checks |
| Normal   | 3                  | 24                | 8                  | 2                  | Disadvantage on *Stealth* checks                               |
| Slow     | 2                  | 18                | 9                  | 3                  | Advantage on *Perception* or *Survival* checks                 |


### Hex Crawl
Exploration of the lands will take place via a hex crawl. The map will be divided into 6 mile hexes and a different travel pace can be chosen for each hex. To be able to choose a pace for a hex you must have travel time available for less than the max travel time for that pace. This means that, on average, you will travel 4 tiles per day at a variety of paces, but it is also possible to travel a total of 5 tiles when moving at a faster pace or only 3 tiles if moving slower. The *Perception*/*Survival* and *Stealth* checks will effect the random combats and encounters you will have during your exploration.
