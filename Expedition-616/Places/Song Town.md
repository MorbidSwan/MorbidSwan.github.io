# Song Town

Song Town is a bustling trade city on the east coast of [Nala](/Expedition-616/Places/Nala) And named after the God of Trade, [Songeop](/Expedition-616/Deities/Songeop). It is home to a diverse cast of merchants, traders, artisans, customers, and everyone in between. If you wanted something, you could find it, or someone who knows how to get it, in Song Town.

While the name Song Town comes from the Songeop, The God of Trade, it is not lost on its citizens that the name also implies a love of music. This resulted in the greatest bard of the time, [Frozzo](/Expedition-616/People/Frozzo), to open [Frozzo's School of Music](/Expedition-616/Places/Frozzo's%20School%20of%20Music).
