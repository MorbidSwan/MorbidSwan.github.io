# Song Town

Song Town is a bustling trade city on the southern coast of [Nala](/Expedition-616/Places/Nala) And named after the God of Trade, [Songeop](/Expedition-616/Deities/Songeop). It is home to a diverse cast of merchants, traders, artisans, customers, and everyone in between. If you wanted something, you could find it, or someone who knows how to get it, in Song Town.

While the name Song Town comes from the Songeop, The God of Trade, it is not lost on its citizens that the name also implies a love of music. This resulted in the greatest bard of the time, [Frozzo](/Expedition-616/People/Frozzo), to open [Frozzo's School of Music](/Expedition-616/Places/Frozzo's%20School%20of%20Music). 

---
## Pre-Cataclysm

### People
While people do live in Song Town, many of the people in the city are only their temporarily peddling their goods to those who have permanent shops. As well, since the city was built by merchants, there is a great diversity in the people who live and travel there. 

### Structure
The beginning of Song Town started with a few merchants deciding to set up shop on the cliffs. With merchants and trade ships in mind, they began to build the city out in rings, leaving cart wide streets in between each ring and double cart wide roads, like spokes of a wheel, that lead to the center where there stands a fountain depicting the God of Trade, Songeop. Shops, homes, inns, and more were in every corner of the city and no more than a few steps away.

### Cargo
Built on the cliffs lining the southern coast of the continent, Song Town employed an impressive system of magic and pullies to hoist cargo from the docks at the bottom of the cliffs to the city proper at the top. The cargo could then be added to a cart that can be pulled down the wide main streets that lead to center of the city.

### Culture
The people often celebrated their diverse heritages with holidays and sales encouraging people to learn more about the merchants and where they hail from.

## Post-Cataclysm

### Separation From Mainland
During the events of [The Cataclysm](/Expedition-616/Events/Cataclysm), many fissures opened across the land. Two of these fissures managed to cross just north of Song Town, pushing it out into the ocean and beyond the horizon.
