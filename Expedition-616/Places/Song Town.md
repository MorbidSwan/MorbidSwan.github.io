# Song Town

Song Town is a bustling trade city on the southern coast of [Nala](/Expedition-616/Places/Nala) And named after the God of Trade, [Songeop](/Expedition-616/Deities/Songeop). It is home to a diverse cast of merchants, traders, artisans, customers, and everyone in between. If you wanted something, you could find it, or someone who knows how to get it, in Song Town.

While the name Song Town comes from the Songeop, The God of Trade, it is not lost on its citizens that the name also implies a love of music. This resulted in the greatest bard of the time, [Frozzo](/Expedition-616/People/Frozzo), to open [Frozzo's School of Music](/Expedition-616/Places/Frozzo's%20School%20of%20Music). 

---
## Pre-Cataclysm

### People
While people do live in Song Town, many of the people in the city are only there temporarily peddling their goods to those who have permanent shops. As well, since the city was built by merchants, there is a great diversity in the people who live and travel there. 

### Structure
The beginning of Song Town started with a few merchants deciding to set up shop on the cliffs. With merchants and trade ships in mind, they began to build the city out in rings, leaving cart wide streets in between each ring and double cart wide roads, like spokes of a wheel, that lead to the center where there stands a fountain depicting the God of Trade, Songeop. Shops, homes, inns, and more were in every corner of the city and no more than a few steps away.

### Cargo
Built on the cliffs lining the southern coast of the continent, Song Town employed an impressive system of magic and pullies to hoist cargo from the docks at the bottom of the cliffs to the city proper at the top. The cargo could then be added to a cart that can be pulled down the wide main streets that lead to center of the city.

### Culture
The people often celebrated their diverse heritages with holidays and sales encouraging people to learn more about the merchants and where they hail from.

## Post-Cataclysm

### Separation From Mainland
During the events of [The Cataclysm](/Expedition-616/Events/Cataclysm), many fissures opened across the land. Two of these fissures managed to cross just north of Song Town, pushing it out into the ocean and beyond the horizon.

### People
The population of Song Town has slowly declined since the events of [The Cataclysm](/Expedition-616/Events/Cataclysm), boasting a population of ~50,000, now sitting at only ~10,000. The people are still as diverse as it was with every person being proud of there heritage, often leading classes or groups to share stories and tales from their family to the younger generations that don't know life outside of Song Town.

### Survival
Being separated from the mainland means that the city is largely safe from the Demons roaming the across it. However, fisherman that have accidentally floated too close to shore have failed to return. It is presumed that some Demons live in the waters near shore, so expedition boats are enchanted with protection and concealment charms taking a little under a year to build and enchant. 

With less land available, agriculture has been limited to many terraced gardens and coops of quail and chickens. Fisherman will take boats out away from the mainland to gather seafood as well.

### Jiuda
On the [Day of Jiuda](/Expedition-616/Events/Jiuda) every year the people of Song Town gather at the northern tip of their now island city at dusk on the summer solstice and watch the [pillar of light](/Expedition-616/Places/The%20Pillar) that streaks out from the horizon. After the flash, and the sun sets, a party is had in the town center in mourning of those that had been lost, celebration of those that lived, and farewell to those traveling on the yearly expedition. A shrine to [Jugeom](/Expedition-616/Deities/Jugeom), The God of Death with a book laid beneath is constructed at the fountain, those who have lost someone from [Jiuda](/Expedition-616/Events/Jiuda) sign their names into the [Death Log](/Expedition-616/Items/Death%20Log).

### Expeditions
On the day after the [Day of Jiuda](/Expedition-616/Events/Jiuda), volunteers leave at the break of dawn on an expedition to the mainland. Their goal is to forge a path to the pillar of light and put an end to the [Jiuda](/Expedition-616/Events/Jiuda). None of these expeditions have ever returned to Song Town, and no information as to their whereabouts exists. As only one expedition leaves per year, the expeditions are number to match the year since the cataclysm. Since 616 years have passed, the current expedition is [Expedition-616](/Expedition-616/People/Expedition-616).
